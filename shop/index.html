Orrery — offline edition

Every Orrery exhibit in one folder that opens with a double-click and needs no internet.

Price: Pay what you want, from $2. The box is pre-filled at $6; pay more if you would like to support the work. Prices are in US dollars; tax, where it applies, is worked out at checkout.

What you get

  • One zip file (about 2 MB). Unzip it, open index.html in a web browser, and everything runs locally: on a laptop, a school computer, a shared drive or a USB stick. Nothing is downloaded or tracked.
  • The gallery, the About page and all 20 exhibits, each with its sources and a plain statement of what its model leaves out.
  • A README.txt with how to open it, and the licences.
  • Each new dated edition we publish is added to your downloads at no extra cost.

What is free anyway

All 20 exhibits are free to read online, and the same files are in our public repository. This edition does not unlock anything. You are paying for one tidy, dated download that works offline, and to support the work.

Licence

Text and figures: CC BY-NC 4.0 (share and adapt with credit to Orrery, including in class, not for sale). Code: MIT.

Good to know

Opens in a current Chromium-based desktop browser such as Chrome or Edge; other browsers should work, but we have not opened it in them. On a phone or tablet, whether the browser opens local files depends on the device.

Orrery teacher pack: Why is summer warm?

Teacher notes with an answer key, and a two-page student worksheet, for the free exhibit Why is summer warm?

Price: $4, fixed price. Prices are in US dollars; tax, where it applies, is worked out at checkout.

What you get

  • Teacher notes, 2 pages: the big idea and the misconception it corrects, level, time and prerequisites, how to run the lesson, answers to questions students may ask, the answer key with the values read from the exhibit, discussion prompts, and curriculum links (named specifications, with the date they were checked).
  • Student worksheet, 2 pages: questions that each name the exhibit’s figure to use.
  • Both as A4 and US Letter PDFs, print-ready in black and white, and a LICENCE.txt.

What is free anyway

The exhibit itself is free online with no sign-up. The student worksheet in the pack is free to print and copy for all your students (its footer says so; not for sale). You are paying for the teacher notes and the answer key, and for the time they save.

Licence

Single-teacher licence: print and copy the pack for your own classes, at school or at home, year after year. Please do not share the teacher notes file or post it online; colleagues can buy their own. This is a request backed by the licence, not copy protection.

Good to know

The pack supports the exhibit; it is not a full curriculum. Curriculum links are to named specifications and are meant as pointers: check them against your own.
